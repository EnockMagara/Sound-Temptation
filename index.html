ABOUT

Temptation is an immersive sound story that explores the complex landscape of human desire and the choices that define us.

Through carefully crafted audio narratives and ambient soundscapes, we journey through moments of tension, decision, and consequence. Each sound tells a story, each silence holds meaning.

This is not just a story about temptation—it's about the human condition, the pull between what we want and what we know is right, the whispers that echo in our minds when we're faced with difficult choices.

Listen carefully. The story unfolds not just in words, but in the spaces between them, in the breath before the decision, in the silence after the choice is made.

THE PROCESS

01

CONCEPTUALIZATION

We began by exploring the psychological aspects of temptation—what drives desire, how choices are made, and the aftermath of decision.

02

SOUND COLLECTION

Recording ambient sounds, whispers, heartbeats, and environmental audio that would create the emotional landscape of the story.

03

NARRATIVE DEVELOPMENT

Crafting the story structure to unfold through horizontal scrolling, allowing listeners to control their journey through temptation.

04

INTERACTIVE DESIGN

Building the seamless audio experience where sound guides the narrative flow and user interaction enhances immersion.

CREATIVE INSIGHTS

"The most powerful temptations are often the quietest ones—the whispers that feel like our own thoughts."

"Sound has the unique ability to bypass our rational mind and speak directly to our emotions and memories."
