ABOUT

Temptation is an intimate audio journey following a man's spiritual struggle on a Sunday morning at 6 AM.

Awakened by his alarm clock, he hears distant church bells calling him to prayer. But his body is heavy, his mind conflicted. Through immersive soundscapes—from soft morning ambiance to whispered temptations—we experience his internal battle between comfort and conviction.

As Satan's voice tempts him to stay in bed longer, promising that "God will understand," our protagonist must choose between the easy path of delay and the difficult path of devotion. In his moment of desperation, crying out "God, please help me," he encounters the gentle, reassuring voice of his Father.

The story unfolds through his morning routine—washing his face, changing clothes, lighting a candle, opening his prayer book—culminating in the powerful recitation of the Lord's Prayer. This is a story about spiritual warfare, divine grace, and the courage to show up imperfectly before God.
